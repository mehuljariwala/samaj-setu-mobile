# Navy and white interaction redesign

User-selected direction: deep navy headers, white forms, bold blue buttons. Replaces the ivory/green/gold direction across the existing prototype, including the new guided biodata flow.

Reference interpretation: supplied medical, ticketing, transit and marketplace screens use strong headers, segmented input areas, distinct action buttons and clear next steps. No external screen content or branding is copied.

Applied design principles:
- Use distinct roles for background, content surface and primary actions, rather than tinting every surface alike.
- Label actions by their outcome; explain registration steps and the effects of admin decisions.
- Separate selected, disabled, error and successful states using text and borders as well as color.
- Preserve Gujarati-first labels and use readable, short guidance adjacent to the control.

Research: https://developer.android.com/codelabs/m3-design-theming and https://www.nngroup.com/articles/button-states-communicate-interaction/

Validation: TypeScript, production build, and inspection of rendered mobile member home and guided form. The prototype remains fictional and uses local-only biodata drafts.
